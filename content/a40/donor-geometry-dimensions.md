Dimensional source of truth for the donor Miata NA front suspension: scanned pickup and ball-joint coordinates, and the stock-vs-narrowed positions I build the frame to. What the numbers mean for the car is in the [geometry design reference](./geometry-design-reference); tyre, ET and fitment are in the [narrowing analysis](./narrowing-analysis).

Build package: 205/65R15 on 15×6 ET38, inner pivots moved 95 mm inboard per side, hub face 655 mm from CL, front track 2 × (655 − 38) = 1234 mm (~1230). See [build constants](./build-constants).

> Plots are generated by [`scripts/plot_geometry_reference.py`](https://github.com/HorvathDawson/portfolio-website/blob/main/scripts/plot_geometry_reference.py).

## Conventions

- **Lateral:** car-frame values are from the vehicle centreline (CL). Ball-joint lateral offsets are measured **inboard from the hub-face plane** (the wheel mounting face). On the donor that plane is 750 mm from CL (710 mm half-track + 40 mm ET).
- **Fore-aft:** forward positive. Ball-joint values are from the hub centre (spin axis).
- **Height:** up positive. Ball-joint values are from the hub centre; pivot heights are from the LCA pivot line.

## Glossary

| Term | What it is |
|------|------------|
| LBJ / UBJ | Lower / upper ball joint. The line through them is the kingpin (steering) axis. |
| LCA / UCA | Lower / upper control arm (A-arm from two inner bushings to the ball joint). |
| KPI | Kingpin inclination: inward lean of the kingpin axis in front view. Steering off-centre would push the wheel below the ground, so the body is lifted instead; the car's weight then pulls the wheel back to centre. |
| ARB | Anti-roll bar. Torsion bar linking left and right. It adds stiffness whenever the two wheels move differently (roll, or one wheel hitting a bump); it does nothing when both wheels move together (equal two-wheel bump). |
| CL | Vehicle longitudinal centreline. |
| Half-track | Miata 1420 / 2 = 710 mm. A40 1234 / 2 = 617 mm. |

## Numbers

The donor front subframe was scanned (Einstar 2) because the forum CAD gave an implausible ~11.5° static caster. **Forum CAD** is the old online/photo-CAD estimate, kept only where the scan measured the same thing. **Value** is what the analysis uses.

Source tags: `scanned` (donor scan), `published` (factory spec or drawing), `derived` (computed from other rows), `build` (chosen value), `estimate` (not yet measured - verify).

### Springs and travel

| Item | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Front motion ratio | **0.680** at an 80° shock (0.690 vertical) | Coilover mount 240 mm out on the LCA; the wheel moves about the front-view instant centre (effective lever 347.8 mm), not the LCA pivot. Replaces the old 0.62-0.63. See [geometry design reference](./geometry-design-reference). |
| Front spring | **38.7 N/mm (221 lb/in)** | 17.9 N/mm wheel rate / 0.680². Spring = wheel rate / MR². |
| Rear spring | 38 / 29 / 22 N/mm | 16.1 N/mm wheel rate at rear MR 0.65 / 0.75 / 0.85. Rear MR still to measure. |
| Front wheel travel | **83 mm bump / 103 mm droop** | 127 mm (5") stroke / 0.680, with 45% of stroke in bump at ride height (assumed). Keeping stock Miata travel instead needs a ~106-112 mm stroke. |

The front-view four-bar is set by four points: LCA pivot, UCA pivot, LBJ and UBJ. Roll centre, instant centre, FVSA, camber gain and motion ratio all follow from those plus the loaded hub height.
