# GAR-009 design-direction mockups

Same content in all three: the rear-suspension post (meta row, work-order card, decision / measure / safety callouts, numbers table with units and status chips, paper figure, formula, code block, sidebar with epics and search, TOC) and the A40 landing page. Copy and numbers are from `content/a40/rear-suspension.md`, `build-constants.md` and the generated constants. These are static HTML mockups, not the app. Fonts are the site's own (Playfair Display, Lora, Space Mono), loaded from local files, plus KaTeX fonts from node_modules for the formula.

Files: `X-post.png` (1440x900, above the fold), `X-post-full.png` (full page), `X-phone.png` (390x844), `X-landing.png` (car landing, full page). `current-post.png` is the live site today. All text/background pairs pass WCAG AA (body ≥ 6:1, chips ≥ 5.8:1), and nothing scrolls sideways at 390 px.

## A — Night garage notebook
The site as it is now, tidied up. Dark navy layers, warm-white ink, gold accent, and the gradient used only for car art, progress bars and the active state. Callouts are coloured shop tags with a punched hole. Tables are ruled engineering sheets with right-aligned tabular numbers and dimmed units. Plots sit on cream paper cards. The car outline sits faintly behind the post title, and the landing hero sweeps gradient ink into the outline.
**Code:** add `tokens.css` (colours, status colours, spacing, radii, type scale) and swap the one-off hex values in blogStyles/garage/roadmapViews. Add a callout plugin for `> [!decision|verify|safety|open|formula]`. Add a table renderer that splits value/unit and maps status to chips, a figure wrapper with a lightbox, and a filename tab and copy button for code blocks. The sidebar stays a list with thin progress bars, and the visitor counter moves to the footer. This is the smallest change of the three.

## B — Blueprint
Same dark base with a faint cyan drafting grid. The post header becomes a drafting title block (drawing no., sheet, rev, date, status, drawn by) in place of the meta row. Each h2 sits under a dimension line with tick ends and a `§ 02` label. Callouts are numbered drafting notes with corner brackets, and Safety gets a hazard-hatch edge. Figures get a crop-frame outline and a "FIG 3 · SCALE NTS" label. On the landing page the gradient car has leader-line annotations (SR20DET, IRS + RX-8, narrowed Miata, WB 2350).
**Code:** everything in A, plus a grid background token and a TitleBlock component that needs a per-post drawing number (new frontmatter field or derived from epic + order). Add h2 decoration in CSS and per-car hero annotation data (points in SVG viewBox coordinates in `car.json`). This is the most "engineering", and the cyan replaces gold as the UI accent.

## C — Workshop zine
Warmer aubergine background with halftone dots and more fun. The work order is a manila shop tag, callouts are tilted stickers with offset shadows, and the plot is taped to the page with masking tape. Headings are Playfair 900 italic with a gradient squiggle and highlighter swash, and there is a handwritten-style margin note. It has the most web-1.0: hazard-stripe "under construction", a glowing odometer counter, 88x31 badges, a news ticker and a starburst "18 posts!" on the landing page.
**Code:** everything in A, plus decoration-only CSS (rotations, tape and burst pseudo-elements). Add an optional `note:` frontmatter for the margin scribble and a ticker fed from the roadmap "next up". Rotations and shadows need print and reduced-motion overrides. This is the least formal, so dense reference posts like build-constants would need a calmer variant (no tilt on tables).

# Round 2 (Nov 2026): D1 / D2, "bay to bench" (B + C, built on the /garage scene)

The owner picked a mix of B (Blueprint) and C (Workshop zine), tied to the animated garage. Both layouts share one design language. The base is the GarageScene night palette with white 2 px line art and siding walls. The neon sign becomes the "home" link, and the car home's hero is a real lit bay, built from GarageScene's own markup and `garageScene.css`. From B: a faint drafting grid on the "bench", a title block (drawing no. A40-FAB-02, sheet 16 of 18, rev, dates, status), dimension-line `§ 02 / 07` dividers and numbered drafting notes. From C: sticker labels on callouts (decision / measure / safety / open), the roadmap tasks as a manila work-order tag with rubber stamps, masking tape on figures, and small web-1.0 gags (under construction, odometer, 88x31 badges). Gold stays the UI accent; the gradient is used for car art and progress bars. Content is real: rear-suspension.md, constants, roadmap "ready now", and sheet numbers by epic then date. Text pairs pass AA (body 11.9:1, smallest meta 4.8:1, stamps ≥ 5:1), and nothing scrolls sideways at 390 px.

Files: `Dx-post.png` (1440x900), `Dx-post-full.png`, `Dx-phone-post.png` (390x844), `Dx-home.png` (car home, full), `D1-phone-home.png` / `D2-phone-home.png` (+ `D2-phone-home-full.png`), plus `D2-index.png` / `D2-phone-index.png` (sheet-index drawer open).

## D1: refined current format
Same three columns as today. The sidebar is the bay wall: neon "THE GARAGE" sign under a roof line, the car switcher as two mini bays (the active one lit with the gradient car, the other with its door half down), epics with sheet counts and roadmap progress bars, the open epic's sheets numbered, a manila "Roadmap" tag and the gags at the foot. The post is a drafting sheet with a title-block header and work-order tag, and the TOC is "On this sheet" with § numbers. The car home has a lit Bay 01 hero, key numbers as a title block, "Next up" work-order tags, the latest sheets as a drawing register, and epic progress.
**Code:** `tokens.css` and the component work that every direction needs (callout plugin, chip tables, figure + lightbox, code tab + copy). Restyle `GarageBlog` sidebar and `CarSwitcher` (the mini-bay thumbnail reuses GarageScene parts), add a `TitleBlock` component, and derive the sheet no. / drawing no. in `postIndex.ts` from epic order + date (optional `drawing:` frontmatter override). Add a `CarHome` for `/garage/:car` that reuses `GarageScene` with one bay, `RoadmapNextUp` data and constants. Routes don't change.

## D2: shop bar + sheet index (new format)
No permanent sidebar. A slim shop bar holds the neon sign (home), mini-bay car switcher, breadcrumb, search, a "Sheets" button and the Roadmap tag. Under it, a **sheet rail** shows all 18 sheets as numbered cells grouped by epic, so any sheet is one click away and you can see where you are. Posts are full drafting sheets with a zone-marked frame, the title block and work order beside the title, a sticky TOC with read progress, and prev/next as **sheet tabs** on the bottom edge. The sheet index opens as a drawer (left on desktop, bottom sheet on phone) with a "you are on" card and every sheet with its date, kind and status. On the phone a **bottom bar** gives prev / Sheets / 16 of 18 / Contents / next. The car home is the bay, then a stats strip and "Continue · sheet 16", then **Next up** tags on a pegboard, then **the wall**: one clipboard per epic with progress, its sheets and its next roadmap task (on phone only the current epic is expanded), then key numbers.
**Code:** everything in D1, plus a new `ShopBar` (replaces the sidebar), `SheetRail`, `SheetIndexDrawer` (dialog with focus trap; same data as the sidebar), `SheetTabs` and `BottomBar` (phone only). Rework `GarageBlog` layout to a single column, and add `CarHome` with `EpicBoard`, which joins posts by epic with roadmap counts and next tasks. Routes don't change. `postIndex.ts` needs stable sheet ordering, and the roadmap data needs a per-epic "next task" selector (the `RoadmapNextUp` ranking filtered by epic). Search moves into the bar and the drawer.

## Recommendation
**D2.** It fits the build-documentation use better. Sheet numbers, the rail and the prev/next tabs make the log readable in order, the wall gives the car home a real "where is the build at" view with next tasks, and the phone gets proper navigation instead of a hidden sidebar. It also gives the reading column the full width. D1 is the lower-risk fallback: same structure, most of the look. The tokens and components are shared, so start with those and switch the layout second.
