## Owner request (Nov 2026)
1. Phones have no hover, so on mobile a tap should give the light effect, with a separate option to then go to the car.
2. Make the stacked phone garage look more industrial (no V roof), and make the desktop scene more industrial too: old gas station or warehouse vibes.

## Design
Same visual language as today: white 2 px line art, night palette, neon, gradient cars, headlight glow. Grittier forms.
- **Desktop (> 600 px): 1950s service station / warehouse.** Flat-roofed building with a parapet and cap flashing (no gable), corrugated or brick-coursed walls, steel roll-up bay doors with a strip of door windows, stencilled bay numbers ("BAY 01"), hazard-stripe edges on the door jambs, an oil-drum or tyre-stack silhouette and a wall lamp per bay. The neon "THE GARAGE" sign moves to a tall gas-station pole sign beside the building (with a small "SERVICE" sub-panel). Interactions unchanged.
- **Phones (≤ 600 px): industrial multi-storey garage.** Flat roof with the neon sign on a rooftop billboard frame (steel lattice), steel I-beam floor slabs with rivets, corrugated walls, stencilled floor numbers, hazard stripes along slab edges, and a freight elevator: open steel cage with a scissor gate on a guide rail instead of the smooth shaft.
- **Phone interaction (tap to light):** scrolling no longer opens floors. Tap a floor → it lights (door up, strip light, gradient car, headlights), the elevator goes to it, and an "Enter ›" button appears on the floor (also: tapping the lit car again enters). Tapping another floor moves the light; tapping outside or pressing Esc turns it off. Enter → existing drive-out → car home (morph), Back → reverse-in with that floor lit. Links stay real `<a href>` (keyboard: focus = lit, Enter = go; ctrl/cmd-click = new tab). Reduced motion: no movement, fades only.
- Car-home hero bay and the shop-bar mini bays restyled to match (same industrial parts).
- **Signage (owner addition):** each car's info (name, year/make, description, chips, post count/updated, "Latest:" link) is an enamel sign plate bolted to the building rather than a plain label band: double-piece plate with corner bolts, painted name, a rule under the year line, status chips as stamped tags with a punched hole, "Latest:" on a smaller bolted plate. Phone: on the wall under each floor; desktop: matching plates under each bay.
- **Gloomier and bloomier (owner, round 2):** darker night with a page vignette falling to near-black, dimmer line art at rest that brightens when a bay lights, soot and faint rain streaks on the walls, low fog along the ground and each floor's deck. Lights bloom: neon sign with layered bloom and a halo on the sky/roof, volumetric cones from the gooseneck lamps and strip lights, headlight beams, the car's colour glowing on the floor and fog, a glowing Enter button and lit floor number. Static gradients plus opacity only; reduced motion keeps the look without flicker.
- **Touch, round 2 (owner):** scroll-driven opening is back: the bay nearest the middle of the screen (the elevator's floor) opens as you scroll, with Enter › on it; tapping the open bay (car or Enter) drives in; tapping a closed bay scrolls to it and opens it, never navigating. No hover needed anywhere on touch; desktop keeps hover.
- **Signs, round 3 (owner: "the signs with info still look ugly"):** a sign carries only the car name plus one short line ("Build log · 18 sheets"); the description becomes one quiet line under the bay/floor; chips, updated date and latest link are dropped from /garage (they're on the car home). Three treatments in a band of wall over each door, switched by a **temporary** `?signs=a|b|c|d` URL param (default `a`) until the owner picks: **A** neon name sign (unlit glass tube at rest, the car's colour with bloom when lit) over a small lightbox; **B** porcelain enamel sign in the car's colour on two chains, double border, chipped edges, catches the lamp when lit; **C** sign-writing painted on the wall, weathered, stencilled detail line, brightens when lit. **D** (owner: "a maybe but b is nice too"): A's neon-tube name as the hero over a small B-style enamel plate (car's accent, double border, chipped edge, small-caps line) on two short chains from the neon's raceway; the plate catches the lamp light and blooms when lit. ## Implementer notes
- **Desktop:** flat roof = brick-coursed parapet with steel cap flashing (overhangs both ends) and a rooftop unit + vent stack behind it. Walls corrugated (`--gs-corrugated`). Each bay: stencilled "BAY 0n" (mono, a mask cuts the stencil bridge), gooseneck wall lamp centred over the door (glows with the bay), door frame with hazard-striped jambs and a coil housing across the top, steel roll-up door with a 6-window strip. Pole sign stands in a reserved column right of the building (`--gs-pole-w`), drawn inside the last bay of the first row so it shares the ground line: neon "THE / GARAGE" panel with inner trim, "SERVICE" sub-panel, steel post on a base plate, tyre stack and oil drum at its foot. The pole is `pointer-events: none`, so it never lights or opens a bay. Gable roof, round vent and roof board are gone everywhere.
- **Desktop rows:** `--gs-cols = min(n, 3)`; bays in later rows get `is-row-start/mid/end` and draw their own parapet piece, so a 4th car is a second roofed building row (checked with 3 and 4 cars).
- **Phone tower:** same parts in a 10:7 floor; flat roof with the neon sign on a steel-lattice billboard frame and a hoist (A-frame + sheave) over the elevator; each storey sits under a riveted I-beam slab with a hazard-striped lower edge (`.gs-bay::before`, runs across the hoistway); big stencilled floor number; freight elevator = guide rails, X-braced hoistway per floor, landing sill and lit floor number, and an open cage with a scissor gate (SVG) that moves with `transform` only.
- **Sign plates:** name (inside the bay link, still its `aria-labelledby` label) + `.gs-bay-plate` (sub, description, meta) drawn as one enamel plate with 4 bolts; sub line ruled off; chips as stamped tags with a punched hole; stats in mono; "Latest:" on a smaller bolted plate. Left-aligned on phones, centred on desktop; 3-to-a-row plates are narrower.
- **Round 2 (owner: gloomier, bloomier, scroll opens on phones)** supersedes the tap-to-light notes below:
  - Touch = `matchMedia('(hover: none), (pointer: coarse)')` OR a `pointerdown` with `pointerType === 'touch'` seen anywhere on the page (so a phone that misreports media still gets touch behaviour; checked by dispatching a touch pointerdown in a mouse context: `gs--touch` appears and floor 01 opens).
  - On touch, a rAF-throttled passive scroll listener opens the stage nearest a probe line (30 % → 75 % of the viewport with scroll progress, so the last floor is reachable) and moves the elevator; ties between side-by-side bays keep the open one. A tap on a closed bay opens it and `scrollIntoView({ block: 'center' })` (smooth unless reduced motion) with scroll picks paused ~0.9 s; a tap on the open bay (car) or its Enter drives out. Outside-tap/Esc unlighting now applies only without touch (scroll decides on touch).
  - Gloom: new tokens `--night-deep`, `--line-art-dim`, `--wall-night`, `--fog`, `--neon-bloom`; `--gs-line` is `--line-art-dim` at rest and `--line-art` on a lit bay; `--gs-grime` (soot under the roof, two-period rain streaks) on walls; darker openings and doors; `.garage-select::before` is a fixed vignette behind the content (iOS-safe, no `background-attachment: fixed`); `.gs-fog` per bay (in front of the door's foot, tinted by the bay's light when on), fog at the pole's foot and on each tower deck.
  - Bloom: `--neon-bloom` text-shadow, a box bloom (`.gs-sign::before`) and a wider halo on the signs; lamp cone (`clip-path` trapezoid + mask) and bulb bloom; strip-light beam (`.gs-beam`); headlight beams (`.gs-lamp::after`, thrown the way the car faces); car-colour glow on the floor and fog; Enter button glow; lit floor number with `--neon-bloom`. All static gradients/shadows toggled by opacity; nothing animates layout.
  - Plates moved to `--sheet` (darker) to sit in the gloom. Measured contrast on the plate (Chromium computed colours): name 12.4, year/make 9.1, description 11.9, stats 7.5, chips 7.5 / 8.4, Latest text 13.7 / link 14.3, elevator floor number (off) 6.7; stencil (decorative) ≈ 9.6 by hand.
  - Safari: masks are `-webkit-` prefixed, no `backdrop-filter`, no `:has()`; `color-mix()`, container units and `clip-path` need Safari 16.2+ (iOS 16.2+). On older Safari the colour-mixed backgrounds drop out (scene still works, just flatter). **WebKit not run:** Playwright WebKit isn't installed here and its download host is blocked by the network policy, so all runs are Chromium with mobile emulation.
- **Tap to light (round 1, replaced by round 2 above on touch):** the scroll-driven floor pick and the in-view IntersectionObserver are removed. State `lit` (index or null). A touch press (`pointerType === 'touch'`, recorded on pointerdown with the lit floor *before* the tap's focus) on an unlit bay's link: `preventDefault`, light it, move the elevator. A touch press on the lit bay's link, or on its "Enter ›" link, drives out. Tapping a floor's plate text lights that floor; a pointerdown outside the bays or Esc unlights (Esc also blurs). Mouse clicks, keyboard Enter and ctrl/cmd/shift/middle clicks are untouched (mouse/keyboard drive out directly, modifier clicks open a tab). Keyboard focus lights the floor on touch/tower (and moves the elevator); focus leaving the bays unlights. On touch, `:hover`/`:focus-within` lighting is disabled (`.gs--touch`) so sticky hover can't light a second floor.
- **Enter button:** a real `<a href>` sibling of the bay link (no nested links), `aria-label="Enter <car>"`, gold, 44 px tall (`--touch`), positioned on the deck/forecourt in stage units (the bay is an inline-size container). Hidden with `visibility` when not lit, so it is not tabbable then.
- **Back:** the returning car reverses in as before; on touch screens its floor stays lit afterwards (Enter showing, elevator there). Desktop mouse: door rolls down after parking, as before.
- **Hero (car home):** same stage parts with the billboard roof ("BAY 0n" neon), no stencil (the sign says it). **Mini bays:** cap flashing strip, hazard jambs, door with window strip; lit bay unchanged otherwise (same view-transition name).
- **Tokens:** all new CSS uses tokens; remaining literals are percentages/cq units/unitless and the scene's own timing variables.
